Platform Features

Everything You Need to
Manage Estate Water

A complete platform built for estate managers, property owners, and billing administrators. Real features from a real, working product.

Smart Metering

Automated water meter readings via IoT devices. No manual readings needed. Real-time consumption data streamed directly to your dashboard.

Estate Dashboard

Complete overview at a glance: meter status, consumption trends, active alerts, and invoice status. Everything in one place.

Property Management

Manage estates, properties, units, owners & tenants. Track individual consumption per property with full audit trails.

Automated Billing

Create billing cycles, generate invoices automatically based on actual consumption and tariff rates. Supports tiered/stepped tariffs and VAT.

Alerts & Notifications

Offline meters, high consumption, and leak detection alerts with configurable severity levels. Never miss a critical issue.

Analytics & Reporting

Month-over-month comparisons, consumption benchmarking, and top consumer reports. Make data-driven decisions with confidence.

User Management

Role-based access for estate managers, property owners, and billing admins. Full audit trail of all actions for complete accountability.
